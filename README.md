# AI SURPLUS FOOD TRACKER
## AI-Powered Surplus Food Rescue & Smart Logistics Platform

> **Reducing food waste through intelligent donation matching, volunteer dispatch, and optimized food rescue logistics.**

---

## 🌍 Problem Statement

Every day, restaurants, bakeries, caterers, and households throw away large quantities of edible food while many NGOs and shelters struggle to meet food demands.

Traditional donation systems are often:
- Manual
- Slow
- Inefficient
- Poorly coordinated

As a result, food expires before reaching those who need it.

**FoodFlow AI** aims to solve this problem by creating an intelligent food rescue network that connects donors, NGOs, and volunteers while optimizing the entire donation process.
